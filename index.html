A round is 15 seconds. Every action spends time units; moving through kelp or debris costs more, diagonals cost half again. Kelp, coral and debris between you and the target can stop a shot. Crouching makes you harder to hit and steadier when firing. Dark water limits sight to 12 tiles — aliens see further.
